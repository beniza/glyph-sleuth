What changed

Six tabs became one field. Variants, properties and font details were never separate modes — they're facets of whatever you just looked up, so they live in a permanent inspector on the right instead of behind a tab you have to leave.

The parse echo under the field shows how your input was read, and what else it could have meant. It teaches the syntax without a help page.

Real data

Every number here came from scanning the 374 faces installed on this machine. Four of them can draw U+2731. Quivira's block coverage and the Dingbats ranking are measured, not sketched.

Language is greyed on purpose — that's SIL langtags/SLDR exemplars answering “which fonts can set this language”, and it's the next thing worth building.
