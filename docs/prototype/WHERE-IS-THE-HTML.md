# Where the prototype HTML went

`Glyph Sleuth.dc.html` and `support.js` live in `site/mockup/` as `index.html`
and `support.js`, served at `/mockup/`. They were briefly at `site/` itself,
until `/` became a generated page.

There is deliberately no second copy here: two copies of a 224 KB single-file
prototype drift, and then nobody knows which one the screenshots match.

Everything else in this folder — `README.md`, `PLAN.md`, `TODO.md`,
`conventions.md`, `screenshots/` — is the spec bundle and stays put. Read
`README.md` for data shapes and behaviour, and [`../BRIEF.md`](../BRIEF.md)
where the two disagree.

**All of it is archival.** It describes the prototype, not the built site. Its
`TODO.md` is the prototype's own open items from 2026-08, not this project's —
open work is on the
[board](https://github.com/users/beniza/projects/10).
